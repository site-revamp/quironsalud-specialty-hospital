# Quirónsalud Specialty Hospital Dubai – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Quirónsalud Specialty Hospital Dubai is a healthcare provider offering specialist medical services across departments including ophthalmology, orthopaedics, cardiology, dermatology, dentistry, physiotherapy and family medicine.

The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting the hospital's specialist services more clearly, strengthening patient confidence through verifiable medical credentials and making appointment enquiries easier for patients in Dubai.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Stronger Homepage Messaging and Appointment Conversion

- Replaces generic introductory messaging with a clearer, patient-focused value proposition.
- Gives greater prominence to specialist medical care and the hospital's key services.
- Improves the visibility of appointment and direct contact actions.

**Issue addressed:** The original homepage used broad messaging such as "Spanish Healthcare Excellence" without clearly communicating the hospital's value or providing a sufficiently prominent appointment pathway.

### 2. More Consistent Specialty Navigation

- Organises medical departments into a clearer and more consistent specialty directory.
- Improves the presentation of services across the homepage.
- Establishes clearer connections between specialties, treatment information and relevant medical professionals.

**Issue addressed:** The original website presented different service lists across its navigation, homepage and Services page, making important departments harder to discover.

### 3. Stronger Trust and Medical Credibility

- Prioritises identifiable medical professionals, their qualifications and relevant experience.
- Emphasises accurate hospital and healthcare network information.
- Improves the visibility of relevant facility, accreditation and insurance information where verified.

**Issue addressed:** The original homepage relied heavily on general claims about healthcare quality and technology, while some group statistics required verification and clearer distinction between hospitals and healthcare centres.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Creates a more professional healthcare-focused appearance that supports the hospital's specialist positioning.
- **Layout and Hierarchy:** Gives greater prominence to the main value proposition, medical specialties and appointment actions.
- **Navigation:** Structures specialist departments more consistently to simplify service discovery.
- **User Experience:** Establishes clearer pathways from exploring medical services to contacting the hospital.
- **Content Consistency:** Aligns specialty information and prioritises accurate, verifiable healthcare details.
- **Overall Direction:** Combines a clear hospital identity with stronger medical credibility, accessible service information and patient-focused communication.

---

**Project Focus:** Specialist service visibility, appointment conversion and healthcare credibility.
