About Quirónsalud

International experience. Personal attention.

Quirónsalud brings its Spanish healthcare heritage and international experience to its specialist hospital in Dubai.

The wider healthcare group has a history spanning more than seven decades. In Dubai, the hospital brings together a range of specialist departments with a focus on personalised treatment and patient care.
